When Ron Pierce joined Imprint in April, his hiring manager described the credit card launch ahead in vivid terms: the long hours, the controlled chaos, the everything-at-once nature of the work. It was framed as both a warning and a sales pitch, and Ron came in expecting heavy work and a steep, fast learning curve. What surprised him was where the velocity actually came from.
