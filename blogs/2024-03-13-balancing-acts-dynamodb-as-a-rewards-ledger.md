Goal At Imprint, we are building rewarding ways to pay and drive customer engagement, acquisition, and brand loyalty. Central to our rewards platform is the ability to track earning of rewards and balances to ensure customers have access to their rewards and we can report back to our partner brands. This post firstly goes over the business needs for retrieving and recording customer balances and rewards and the design of an optimized DynamoDB table schema that satisfied the business requirements.
